# Support

For a reproducible problem with DebtShark, use the bug-report template. For a product idea, use the feature-request template.

DebtShark is an educational, local-only organizer. This repository cannot provide individual legal, financial, tax, credit, or debt-collection advice. If a notice, lawsuit, or deadline affects you, contact an appropriate qualified professional or local legal-aid organization.

When requesting help, never share real account numbers, addresses, notices, documents, or other identifying financial information.
