# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DebtShark exists to make a stressful subject clearer and more humane. We commit to a welcoming, respectful space for everyone who participates, regardless of background, identity, experience, or financial circumstances.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while making room for correction.
- Protect privacy. Never post real account numbers, notices, financial details, court documents, addresses, or other personal information.
- Focus discussion on improving the project and its users’ experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sharing others’ private information, or using the project to market financial products or legal services are not welcome.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer privately through the [repository owner’s GitHub profile](https://github.com/boredpolymath). Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or limit participation when needed to protect the community.
